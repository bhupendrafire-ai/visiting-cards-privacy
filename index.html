Visiting Cards Privacy Policy

Effective 10 August 2026 · Developer: Bhupendra Warade

Visiting Cards is designed to keep your business-card catalogue under your control. The app has no Visiting Cards account, advertising, analytics, or subscription. Core card recognition and catalogue functions run on your device. A small developer-operated image service is used only when you choose to exchange your own card's selected background.

Information handled by the app

Depending on the features you use, the app may access and store on your device:

  • photographs of business cards;
  • text recognised from cards, including names, job titles, companies, phone numbers, email addresses, websites, postal addresses, GSTIN/PAN details, and other printed information;
  • scan location, place name, date, and event information;
  • voice-note audio and text you attach to a card;
  • follow-up reminders, tags, interaction notes, and app settings;
  • the contact details, layout, and visual background you choose for “My Card”; and
  • an optional API key that you provide for an AI service.

How information is used

The app uses this information to read cards, let you review and correct them, organise your catalogue, search it, group cards by company, place, event, or type, set reminders, and create exports or backups that you request.

Optional services that can receive data

Core card reading works without an AI service. If you enable AI-assisted reading and save your own provider key, relevant card text is sent directly from your device to the provider you select: OpenRouter, Anthropic, OpenAI, or Google Gemini. A card photograph is included only if you separately enable “Also send the card photo.” Your selected provider processes that request under its own terms and privacy policy.

If you enable company lookup, the app may open the public website printed on a card to identify what that company does. It does not send your catalogue to that website.

If you ask the app to record where a card was scanned, the app obtains a device location and may use the operating system’s geocoding service to turn coordinates into a place name. That service may process the coordinates under the platform provider’s privacy terms.

If you enable My Card exchange, your contact fields and layout recipe are placed in the QR shown or shared by you. The app uploads the selected visual background over HTTPS to the Visiting Cards exchange service backed by Vercel Blob so the receiving phone can download and cache it. For a card created in the app, that blob contains no structured contact fields. If you choose an existing card image, the image itself may visibly contain the text printed on that card. The service stores no account, original filename, contact database, or QR payload. A one-way hash of the app installation identifier is used only to enforce the upload rate limit.

Exports, backups, and contact saving occur only when you request them. The selected destination app or service then handles the information under its own privacy terms.

Storage, retention, and deletion

Your catalogue and ordinary card media remain on your device until you delete records, clear the app’s storage, or uninstall the app. You can delete cards, contacts, and companies inside the app. Copies that you export, back up, or send to another person remain with their destination and must be deleted there separately.

Each exchanged background is stored under an unguessable address. A separate deletion secret stays in secure storage on the uploading phone and is never placed in the QR. The one-way installation hash and rate counter are automatically deleted within 48 hours. A receiving phone may retain its downloaded copy until its owner deletes that card or app data.

An optional API key is stored using Android Keystore-backed secure storage. Network requests to optional providers and websites use encrypted HTTPS connections.

Request data deletion

Visiting Cards has no user account. To delete data handled by the developer-operated exchange service:

  1. Edit or delete My Card in the app. The app uses its device-held deletion secret to remove the previous hosted background and its server metadata.
  2. If the app or deletion secret is no longer available, email bhupendrafire@gmail.com with the public background URL from the saved or shared QR/card and ask for deletion.

Manual requests are completed within 30 days. The hosted background and its server metadata are deleted. Rate-limit records expire automatically within 48 hours. Copies already downloaded by another user cannot be remotely removed; that user can delete the received card or clear the app's data. Local catalogue data can be deleted inside the app or by clearing Visiting Cards storage in Android settings.

Sharing and sale

The developer does not sell, rent, analyse, or use your catalogue or exchanged backgrounds for advertising. The app contains no advertising or analytics SDK. Data is disclosed to an optional service only as described above or when you intentionally export or share it.

Permissions

The app may request camera, microphone, location, contacts, and notification permissions to provide the corresponding feature. You can deny or revoke these permissions in Android settings. The app uses Android’s system photo picker for gallery selection.

Children

Visiting Cards is a business productivity tool intended for adults and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the app’s data practices change. The effective date above will be revised when that happens.

Questions about this policy can be sent to bhupendrafire@gmail.com.
