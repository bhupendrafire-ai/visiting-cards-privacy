Visiting Cards Privacy Policy

Effective 2 October 2026 · Developer: Bhupendra Warade

Visiting Cards is designed to keep your business-card catalogue under your control. Android card recognition and catalogue browsing work on your device without signing in. Optional Google sign-in creates a Visiting Cards account for approved cross-device catalogue sync; the web trial requires approved sign-in. The app has no advertising. Online services and technical SDK diagnostics are described below.

Request deletion of your account or cloud data — no app installation is required.

Information handled by the app

Depending on the features you use, the app may access and store on your device:

  • photographs of business cards;
  • text recognised from cards, including names, job titles, companies, phone numbers, email addresses, websites, postal addresses, GSTIN/PAN details, and other printed information;
  • scan location, place name, date, and event information;
  • voice-note audio and text you attach to a card;
  • follow-up reminders, tags, interaction notes, and app settings;
  • the contact details, layout, and visual background you choose for “My Card”; and
  • an optional API key that you provide for an AI service.

How information is used

The app uses this information to read cards, let you review and correct them, organise your catalogue, search it, group cards by company, place, event, or type, set reminders, and create exports or backups that you request.

Optional services that can receive data

Google sign-in and Firebase catalogue sync

When you choose Google sign-in, Google/Firebase Authentication processes your sign-in and assigns an account identifier. Visiting Cards stores your Google account name, email address, profile-photo URL, account identifier, approval status, and related timestamps in Firebase to manage your account and closed-trial access. A request for trial access can be stored even if access is not yet approved. Firebase Authentication also processes IP addresses and technical request information for authentication, security, and abuse prevention.

If you approve catalogue sync, Google Cloud Firestore and Cloud Storage for Firebase receive your catalogue and associated media: card/contact/company details, postal addresses and phone numbers, scan locations and place names, event and follow-up information, interaction notes, private text notes, voice recordings, card photographs, logos, and My Card data. Sync also stores revision information, timestamps, content hashes, and a generated device identifier used to coordinate changes. Previously linked, signed-in devices can sync automatically. The app retains a working local copy for offline use.

Firebase is a service provider for this account and sync functionality. Catalogue sync is protected by encrypted network connections and account access controls, but is not end-to-end encrypted: it is distinct from the password-encrypted backups and key vault below. Private notes are included in account sync and full backups even though contact sharing excludes them.

Encrypted API-key vault

If you choose cross-device access to your AI key, the app encrypts that key on your device with a separate password before saving it in your account's Firestore key vault. The vault stores the encrypted key, random salt/nonce, authentication tag, encryption-version information, owner account identifier, and update time. The vault password and unencrypted key are not sent to Firebase by this feature. Your password is required to unlock the key on another device; the developer cannot recover it. When you use an AI provider, the app separately sends the unencrypted API key to that selected provider over HTTPS to authenticate the request.

Google Drive recovery backups

Google Drive backup is separate from Firebase account sync. If you connect Drive and enable backups, the app stores password-encrypted recovery files in a backup folder in your Google Drive, using Google's sign-in and file APIs. Full backups can include the catalogue, notes, and media. Restoring an encrypted backup on another device requires the original backup password. Google processes the account and file metadata needed to provide this service. Older unencrypted ZIP backups, if present, are not made encrypted retroactively. Disconnecting Drive does not delete files already backed up.

Other optional services

Core card reading works without an AI service. If you enable AI-assisted reading and save your own provider key, relevant card text is sent directly from your device to the provider you select: OpenRouter, Anthropic, OpenAI, or Google Gemini. A card photograph is included only if you separately enable “Also send the card photo.” Your selected provider processes that request under its own terms and privacy policy.

If you enable company lookup, the app may open the public website printed on a card to identify what that company does. It does not send your catalogue to that website.

If you ask the app to record where a card was scanned, the app obtains a device location and may use the operating system’s geocoding service to turn coordinates into a place name. That service may process the coordinates under the platform provider’s privacy terms.

If you enable My Card exchange, your contact fields and layout recipe are placed in the QR shown or shared by you. The app uploads the selected visual background over HTTPS to the Visiting Cards exchange service backed by Vercel Blob so the receiving phone can download and cache it. For a card created in the app, that blob contains no structured contact fields. If you choose an existing card image, the image itself may visibly contain the text printed on that card. The service stores no account, original filename, contact database, or QR payload. A one-way hash of the app installation identifier is used only to enforce the upload rate limit.

Exports and contact saving occur when you request them. Backups can also run automatically after you enable scheduled backup. The selected destination app or service handles the information under its own privacy terms.

Recognition SDK diagnostics

Android text recognition and barcode scanning use Google ML Kit on the device. This local recognition does not require uploading your card photograph to Google. The ML Kit SDK does collect technical information such as device and app versions, installation identifiers, feature events, configuration, input/output sizes, error codes, and performance measurements for diagnostics and usage analytics. These technical transmissions use HTTPS and do not require Firebase catalogue sync to be enabled. The app does not offer a separate switch to turn off this SDK telemetry. See Google's ML Kit data disclosure and Firebase privacy information.

Storage, retention, and deletion

You can delete cards, contacts, and companies inside the app. Deleted records remain recoverable in the app's Trash for 30 days unless you delete them permanently sooner. Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes local app data, not cloud copies. Keep an export or backup of anything you want to retain before deleting it.

Firebase retains account/trial records, synced catalogue snapshots and media, and an optional encrypted key vault until they are removed or you request cloud-data/account deletion. A successful sync can propagate record changes and attempt to remove media no longer referenced by the current catalogue; earlier catalogue revisions and files whose cleanup failed can remain. Deleting a record locally is therefore not a guarantee that every historical cloud copy has been erased. Signing out stops that session but does not delete the cloud account or its stored data. Removing an API key from local settings does not remove its encrypted cloud-vault copy.

Google Drive recovery files, local backup/safety copies, exports, contacts saved to the phone, and copies sent to other people have separate lifetimes and must be deleted at their destinations. Cloud-account deletion does not remotely erase those copies. Sign out on linked devices before requesting deletion; signing in and enabling sync again can create a new account or upload retained local data again.

Each exchanged background is stored under an unguessable address. A separate deletion secret stays in secure storage on the uploading phone and is never placed in the QR. The one-way installation hash and rate counter are automatically deleted within 48 hours. A receiving phone may retain its downloaded copy until its owner deletes that card or app data.

An optional API key is stored using Android Keystore-backed secure storage. Network requests to optional providers and websites use encrypted HTTPS connections.

Request data deletion

To request deletion of your Visiting Cards account and associated Firebase data, email bhupendrafire@gmail.com from the Google email address used for the app, with the subject “Visiting Cards account and data deletion.” You can do this without installing or opening the app. You can also reach this page through Settings → Privacy policy.

We verify control of the account before acting. Do not send passwords, API keys, your vault password, or your catalogue. If you cannot access that email address, contact the same inbox for identity-verification help. Verified requests are completed within 30 days. Account deletion removes the Firebase Authentication account, profile, trial request/access records, all associated Firestore documents (including the encrypted key vault), and the user's Cloud Storage catalogue revisions and media. You can instead request deletion of only the cloud catalogue/media or only the key vault while keeping the account; specify that scope in your email.

We confirm when deletion is complete. We do not keep the deleted catalogue or vault for advertising or analytics. Support correspondence necessary to process and document your request is separate from app catalogue storage. Your Google account itself is not deleted. Google Drive files, local copies, exports and copies received by others are not automatically deleted by this request; remove them at their destinations.

To delete data handled by the developer-operated exchange image service:

  1. Edit or delete My Card in the app. The app uses its device-held deletion secret to remove the previous hosted background and its server metadata.
  2. If the app or deletion secret is no longer available, email bhupendrafire@gmail.com with the public background URL from the saved or shared QR/card and ask for deletion.

Manual requests are completed within 30 days. The hosted background and its server metadata are deleted. Rate-limit records expire automatically within 48 hours. Copies already downloaded by another user cannot be remotely removed; that user can delete the received card or clear the app's data. Local catalogue data can be deleted inside the app or by clearing Visiting Cards storage in Android settings.

Sharing and sale

The developer does not sell, rent, or use your catalogue or exchanged backgrounds for advertising or behavioural analytics. The app contains no advertising SDK or Firebase Analytics integration. The technical diagnostics performed by Google's SDKs are described above. Data goes to the service providers and optional services described here, or to destinations you intentionally choose for export or sharing.

Permissions

The app may request camera, microphone, location, contacts, and notification permissions to provide the corresponding feature. You can deny or revoke these permissions in Android settings. The app uses Android’s system photo picker for gallery selection.

Children

Visiting Cards is a business productivity tool intended for adults and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the app’s data practices change. The effective date above will be revised when that happens.
